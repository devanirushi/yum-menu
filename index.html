Lunch, sorted

Yum Menu

One main meal a day, with one day a weekend out for lunch. Every plate is built to be filling: a protein, a gluten-free base, a big salad or vegetables, and a good fat. All vegetarian, light on spice, cooked in ghee or olive oil.

How the week works

Menus run Monday to Sunday and arrive by Friday night for the following week, so you can order on the weekend. One weekend day is lunch out. The two tabs cover Monday 2 to Sunday 8 November and Monday 9 to Sunday 15 November.

Good to know

Every plate is built to be filling. New dishes have a recipe in the Recipes tab; dishes you already make do not.

Protein is a rough estimate for Rushi / Dipali. A plan for the week, not a rule.
