Lunch, sorted

Yum Menu

One main meal a day, with one day a weekend out for lunch. Every plate is built to be filling: a protein, a gluten-free base, a big salad or vegetables, and a good fat. All vegetarian, light on spice, cooked in ghee or olive oil.

How the week works

Menus run Monday to Sunday and arrive by Friday night for the following week, so you can order on the weekend. One weekend day is lunch out. This menu covers 12 to 25 October, then a break, and starts again Monday 2 November.

What changed after your feedback

Meals are bigger and fuller. Zucchini boats are gone, and the pulao now comes with paneer tikka and a chana salad. New this time: a non-wheat pizza, tofu scramble brunch, kati rolls and a wheatless tortilla. Where tempeh is hard to find, use tofu or paneer. New dishes have a recipe in the Recipes tab; dishes you already make do not.

Protein is a rough estimate for Rushi / Dipali. A plan for the week, not a rule.
